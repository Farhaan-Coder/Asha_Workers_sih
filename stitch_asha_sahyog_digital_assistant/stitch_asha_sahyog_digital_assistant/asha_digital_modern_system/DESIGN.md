# Design Rationale
- **Trust & Authority:** Deep navy blue (#002147) for headers and primary actions.
- **Accessibility:** High contrast ratios, large touch targets (min 48px), and bold standard system fonts.
- **Tricolor Identity:** Subtle saffron and green accents used for priority states and active navigation.
- **Voice-First UI:** Prominent microphone icons integrated into every input field to support varied digital literacy levels.
